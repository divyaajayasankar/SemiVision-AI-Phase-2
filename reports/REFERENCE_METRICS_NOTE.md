# Reference Metric Provenance

The values in `reference_final_metrics.csv` and `results/reference_run/` come from the previously verified full project execution supplied in the research history. They are retained as archived evidence and are not represented as newly recomputed in this packaging environment.

Fresh executions write separate outputs to `results/phase1/`, `results/phase2/`, `results/ablation/`, `results/drift/`, and `reports/final_metrics.csv`.
